# Source-only Gerber U05

The connected-copper profile treats a pad and its source-connected trace as one
physical conductor. Only different frozen original copper components impose
ordinary inter-component clearance. Source-bound functional channels and
protected pin slots impose additional separation even within one physical
component or net. Existing pad-to-trace joints remain integral; common-component
membership never authorizes a new local pin bridge. Ownership never changes
because candidate growth touches another component. Original holes/notches and
intentional pin gaps remain protected. T04 and T05 absorb qualified
growth-created pockets into one reusable original-owner surface. Every complete
logical repair is checked before splitting or clipping; a rejected protected-slot
fill cannot return as fragments, another radius or a later-stage repair. The
20% allowance does not override protected separation.

Run `tools/Run_Gerber_U05.command --input-dir "/absolute/raw/directory" --gerber "top.art.gbr"`.
Selection must be explicit. A filename selects artwork; it does not establish X2
stack identity. No KiCad input is invented. This separate route does not replace
the existing KiCad/native machine-audit entry points or their acceptance modes.

Each fresh job captures source hashes and clearance/remedy registries. It first
regenerates the conservative legacy reservation reference from source using
T01, T03, T04, T02, T05 and the versioned local-remedy recipe. It then constructs
the connected-copper sequence. No historical generated result is a generation
input. A process read fence rejects historical evidence reads outside that fresh
job and its immutable source tree.

Every stage retains complete construction and two fresh outcome reconstructions.
Gerber T04 uses a deterministic ten-rung widening ladder. The global provisional
registry is `contracts/t04/settings.json`: the previously practiced maximum
factor is 1.5x, with ten 0.05-factor increments, and total source-width increase
is at most 0.030 mm (30 um, not 30 um per side). Both limits apply. An excessive
rung is rejected without clamping to an off-ladder width. Each job captures
`T04_SETTINGS_SNAPSHOT.json` and binds it to its producer.

Each eligible route proposes its next rung independently in a fixed order, once
per pass, against the current scene. Every candidate uses original source
geometry and width; offsets never compound. There are at most ten complete
passes and ten attempts per route, with early termination when no route advances.
There is no grouped shared-factor search, combination search, backtracking or
binary refinement in this Gerber T04 path. Groups and graphs remain for spatial
dependencies, bounded caches and invalidation of affected results. Unchanged
verified dependencies are reused; complete final validation remains mandatory.

Each fresh T04 evaluator checks the attempt-ledger hashes, rung and width limits,
source admission, complete nominal/envelope final geometry, and all recorded
accept/reject predicates. Per-route evidence reports final rung, original and
resulting width, percentage growth and blocking reason. Acceptance proves safe
completion of the prescribed ladder, not a continuous or global maximum. Existing
smoothing and complete logical-fill checks remain, and later remedy/prior copper
must not bypass a rejected width decision. T05 retains all source attachments
and complete assembly checks. The historical KiCad/native WidthAdapter and its
operator acceptance mode are separate from this Gerber T04 migration.

Export preserves source commands and uses the existing 1 nm file grid. A cut-in
is retained only when its parsed integer boundaries reproduce the complete native
material exactly. Rounded hole bridges that alter that material are rejected; an
exact axial bridge is tried within a fixed resource budget. Where a faithful
legal axial cut-in is unavailable, constrained triangulation emits equivalent
simple positive regions under the same source identity. Exact integer area and
signed atomic boundary equality prove no hole was filled and no positive triangle
was omitted. Export/reparse, all source/scalar/attachment/clearance/fidelity and
physical partition checks, deliberate corruptions and the remaining-defect audit
must pass before delivery. The independent whole-image reference recompiles
every intermediate positive contribution as well as the final scene, matching
the append-only source-stage contract without reading the generated file ledger.
The actual-file verifier proves every parsed region against the independently
compiled native parent inventory before assembling complete physical copper.
Exact integer boundaries and area, complete part indices, source/owner identity
and all holes must match. Only then are internal triangle edges removed from
the physical assembly; stable integer ring and operand order makes it independent
of region count and floating precision tags. The raw unwelded overlay metric is
retained as a diagnostic. The physical 2 nm boundary limit, production clearance,
source preservation, scope, connectivity and negative controls remain gates.
Both fresh native workers bind the resulting actual-material image used by the
final rendering and remaining-defect audit. No source or generated file is edited
by this verification step.

For connected T05, the exporter first constructs the complete local physical
target from the existing owner plus qualified addition, clips only its redundant
owner support to the local window, and retains the complete addition. All four
original support/coverage/equivalence/scope predicates and their original area
epsilon must pass; otherwise the existing redundant owner-overlap forms apply.
The compact local surface must still meet the unchanged per-attachment region
budget. This changes only redundant representation of already qualified copper.
If both original overlap forms fail their physical-equivalence predicates, the Gerber-only route can
serialize the qualified addition directly. This avoids intersection vertices
introduced solely by duplicated existing copper. All four original support,
addition, physical-union and scope predicates retain the same area epsilon;
the actual parsed-file tip fidelity and complete physical checks remain mandatory.
The KiCad export route and its overlap construction are unchanged.

The alternate global-overlay diagnostic can contain valid zero-area lines in
addition to its positive polygons. Diagnostic component counting uses the existing
areal-context extraction and records that representation explicitly. Every
positive face is retained, including minimum-grid faces; invalid geometry still
halts. Actual candidate copper continues to require valid polygons, unchanged
owner topology, source containment and the complete foreign-clearance checks.
This changes no candidate material. Raw T05 results are captured before the
remedy, and incremental visual receipts distinguish reused evidence from new work.

Large contour metrics use all vertices and bounded
spatial indexes, never display sampling or simplification.

All generation, logs, tests and caches require the prepared LaCie APFS image;
there is no internal fallback. Source/old outputs stay immutable. Delivery creates
a new sibling directory containing only the selected Gerber filename and
`final_render.png`. Green always highlights growth relative to the original.
Detailed comparisons and rejected controls remain in the job's visual bundle.

The default nominal clearance is 0.040 mm, the construction gap 0.0401 mm.
The registered owner allowance is not consumed by this route. Per-job snapshots
prevent an on-the-fly registry change from altering an active job.

Prior `.DS_Store` custody FAIL remains explicit, unwaived, with writer unattributed.
Machine/geometry qualification and an approved preview do not establish owner,
independent, electrical or manufacturing release acceptance. Coverage recovery
continues separately; no recovery files or failed jobs are reset/relabelled.

## Incremental qualification

After a T05/export correction, use
`python -B -m src.gerber_u05.incremental --prefix-run "/absolute/accepted/connected/prefix"`.
This creates a separate job; a stopped or failed original job is never resumed.
The original source, source-derived reference and completed T01/T03/T04/T02
construction plus both evaluations are retained with their exact input, policy,
producer, output, search-witness, process and audit hashes. Changed upstream
algorithms or policies invalidate reuse. Named exact metric/storage equivalences
are explicit; every other upstream producer must match. New T05 construction and
both evaluations, actual export/reparse, all corruptions, remaining-defect audit
and final rendering remain mandatory. Reports label prefix reuse rather than
claim another full fresh source run. Distinct batch inputs use the full route.

The prior reference is minimum copper at T05 entry, not merely a reservation for
foreign components. Its missing material is absorbed into the existing original
owner surfaces before planning new junction growth. Both current and qualified
prior foreign surfaces constrain every new spindle. A one-grid construction
containment guard protects the unchanged two-grid native fidelity limit during
rounding. The existing native foreign-clearance, source-hole and final prior
retention checks remain gates; adding old and new shapes blindly is prohibited.
This avoids replacing an earlier tall rectangle with only a wider, shorter one
and then allowing a new foreign junction to occupy its former clearance space.
