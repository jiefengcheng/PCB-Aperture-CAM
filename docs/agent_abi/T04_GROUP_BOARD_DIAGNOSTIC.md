# Explicit T04 full-board construction diagnostic

The later [residual-chain consolidation](T04_RESIDUAL_CHAIN_HANDOFF.md) remains
bounded and opt-in. Its evidence does not itself enable this entry or constitute
a new board-readiness receipt. Consolidating this module does not launch it.

The owner authorized a full-board T04 test on 2026-11-20 after accepting the
five cases and the bounded 64-route optimization. This is a new explicit
diagnostic scope for that parallel-group caller, not the older checked-profile
`--whole-board` algorithm and not completed-scene or export acceptance.

`python -m src.gerber_u05.group_board_job --accepted-t03 <job> --output <new-job>
--qualification <fresh-readiness.json>` first verifies code-bound fresh five
and 64-route component results, exact accepted-copper/report parity, test-log
integrity and unchanged accepted T03/policies. It exclusively creates a new
attempt, retaining failures without retry or resume. Existing bounded and
checked-profile entry gates remain intact.

The group caller selects every admitted native route internally; it accepts no
route subset in `FULL_BOARD_DIAGNOSTIC`. Every native admission disposition is
accounted for, including covered KEEP paths and bulk-only roles. Unknown,
duplicate, empty or inconsistent native inventories stop execution. Covered
occurrences and bulk roles retain the established eligibility rules. No
cache budget or the bounded 128-route limit removes eligible work.

The same straight, maximal-arc, residual/default, joint, skin and tangent
algorithms run. Constructed groups remain fixed supports with zero geometry
audits. Existing reference, residual functional, bulk recovery, native-route
connection, differential-pocket and preservation failures stop the diagnostic.
Remaining endpoint obligations and unresolved corner records are reported;
they are not promoted into a completed T04 claim. Source caps, local 25% rule
and the seven-pass schedule are unchanged.

The full-board result has `full_board_run: true`, `scope:
FULL_BOARD_DIAGNOSTIC`, `publication_ready: false`, `output_scene_ref: null`,
`complete_t04: false`, `replay: false` and `export: false`. Canonical output is
streamed to gzip without allocating a second full-board JSON blob. Raw and
compressed hashes bind the retained construction. All jobs, logs, immutable
executable snapshots and preparation data remain on LaCie.

On a numerical failure, the job also retains available skin/opening/overlay
operands under `failure_operands/`, with exact WKB hashes, precision, SRID and
native joint identity. Capture does not re-execute the failing operation or
change its failure disposition. The original exception remains the primary
error if operand capture itself is unavailable.
