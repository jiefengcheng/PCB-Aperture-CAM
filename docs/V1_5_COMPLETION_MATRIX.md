## Numerical contract frozen before evaluation

New composition target: maximum circular chord sag 1e-6 mm; conservative expanded-path displacement budget 1e-5 mm including coordinate quantization and transforms. FC-1 keeps its existing 1e-5 mm linear / 1e-4 mm² area obligations unchanged. Source-anchor association: 0.002 mm as an explicitly experimental native-export mapping allowance, not a factory rule. No-edit layer comparison requires mutual shape containment within 1e-5 mm and symmetric-difference area bounded by combined boundary length times that numerical allowance; topology and nonempty/empty status also match. Nominal intersection comparison uses the same shape criterion. No independent best fit, geometry buffer(0), make_valid repair or tolerance inflation to conceal a mismatch.

The raw polygonization of legal Gerber contours is interpretation, not permission to repair arbitrary invalid contours: unsupported transverse self-intersections must remain NOT_ESTABLISHED. Local macro/AB clear operations compose locally before placement; layer clear operations affect the ordered global image. Include unit, role, polarity and format coverage before measurements.

No technical profile PASS can omit a required file/check or absorb required unknowns. Overall V1.5 electrical/process unknowns remain visible even if the declared geometry/preservation subprofile passes. Acceptance-set changes require an explicit versioned decision before the affected evaluation, never a post-hoc reclassification.
