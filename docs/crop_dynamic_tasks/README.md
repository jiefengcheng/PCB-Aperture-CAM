## 5. One working scene, not independent edits against ORG

Preserve two distinct representations:

**ORG truth:** pinned original objects, geometry, provenance, observed contacts/separations, and protected obligations.

**Working scene:** accepted experimental replacements/additions, scene revision, source-contribution map, spatial lookup, and trace reservations.

For each selected feature or small joint patch:

```text
read current scene and all affecting neighbors
→ construct a nominal proposal from ORG, current state, and frozen policy
→ adapt the proposal against current copper and reserved future work
→ check the complete atomic patch
→ accept into a new disposable scene revision, or KEEP/ABSTAIN/REJECT
→ update/invalidate affected spatial entries and save the event
```

Proposal and decision must be interleaved at feature/patch scale. Do not generate every feature against the unchanged source and label the resulting conflicts a dynamic algorithm.

The scope is the whole crop, but the system may process small interacting patches internally. Use interaction edges from overlapping proposed influence regions/reservations and relevant geometry, not repeated-family membership. A physical component is not an edit-permission set or proof that all of its branches may be joined.

### Growth and priorities

For eligible land-like targets, use a bounded ORG-derived influence region, a protected retained core, and a nominal growth cap. Respect protected original copper, current accepted neighbors, explicit gap policy, fixed ports and routing reservations, and unknown influences.

Apply separating-gap rules to the relationships for which they were declared: distinct original physical components and any specifically protected same-component spaces. Do not impose an insulating gap between a land and its intended attached trace. Conversely, belonging to the same connected component does not authorize arbitrary new branches, shortcuts or filling every internal void. Unknown `net 0` assignments are not one shared electrical net.

Optional growth may be clipped or reduced; do not shrink an original protected core merely to accommodate a later enlargement. Keep growth attached to its intended feature. Do not fill distant trace-only voids, add detached islands, or bridge separate components.

A round-preference heuristic is allowed where selected from ORG evidence and feasible. It is not a requirement to keep exactly one round feature in every component. Priority is a declared demo policy, not recovery of the expert's historical order.

Use one deterministic main order and at most one simple alternative. Before circular growth, reserve the protected bodies of noncircular features and the intended finite-width trace corridors, even if their transformation is scheduled later.

### Bounded reconciliation

A later width/path/junction conflict may reopen optional earlier growth through an explicit local transaction. Limit this to one local reconciliation pass per affected patch in the initial implementation. Preserve the earlier attempt and reason. If the declared constraints still conflict, retain a safe prior patch or abstain. Never allow unlimited shrinking cascades or silent protected-object changes.

### Directional fit, not exact EDIT reconstruction (**final T06 goal, not T01 gate**)

On the fixed qualified comparison domain Q, use composed copper:

```text
O = ORG copper; T = observed EDIT copper; C = exported candidate copper
D0 = area(O symmetric_difference T)
D1 = area(C symmetric_difference T)
relative_improvement = (D0 - D1) / D0, when D0 is nonzero
```

The default research objective is **at least 20% reduction of D0**, together with the crop-scale objectives and hard invariants. This is a proposed engineering demo target, not a manufacturing tolerance or a result already achieved. Adopt/freeze it before new fitting; do not lower it after a weak result. If the target cannot be qualified or the objective is missed, report that separately instead of replacing it with a presentation PASS.

Always also report correctly/incorrectly added copper, correctly/incorrectly removed copper, missed additions/removals, unchanged-area damage, actual width/gap profiles, and the fraction of V excluded from Q. Large unchanged areas must not dominate the score. If Q is partial, the fit claim is partial; no complete-crop reconstruction claim follows.

Compare unchanged ORG, static-neighbor baseline, dynamic main order, one alternative order, meaningful stage prefixes, and previous recipes only where their domains genuinely match. Prefix fit need not improve monotonically; final acceptance is separate.
